Please read this notice before entering Government Printing Works premises. It explains how visitor information is handled and the security requirements applicable to all visitors.

01

Visitor Information & POPIA

Government Printing Works (GPW) collects and processes visitor information to manage access to its premises and maintain a safe, secure environment. Personal information is handled in accordance with the Protection of Personal Information Act, 2013 (POPIA). Where consent is the applicable lawful basis, GPW will request your consent before processing your information.

Your information will be used for:

Verifying your identity and visitor details
Registering your visit and managing access to GPW premises
Communicating with you about your visit, where necessary
Maintaining visitor access and security records

How your information is protected

GPW will take appropriate measures to safeguard your personal information against unauthorised access, loss, misuse or disclosure. Access will be limited to authorised personnel and service providers who require the information for legitimate purposes. Information may be disclosed where necessary for visitor management, security or compliance with applicable law.

Retention and your rights: Visitor records will be retained only for as long as necessary for the stated purposes or as required by applicable law and approved retention policies. You may request information about the processing of your personal information and exercise your rights under POPIA, subject to applicable legal requirements.
02

Security Notice

Government Printing Works Visitor Access Control

The following security procedures apply to all visitors:

  1. Visitor details will be recorded manually or electronically and protected in accordance with POPIA.
  2. Visitors must present acceptable proof of identity, such as a South African identity document, driver’s licence or passport. Entry will not be permitted without satisfactory identification.
  3. Security screening and searches of persons, vehicles, bags and luggage may be required on entry to or exit from the premises, in accordance with applicable law and GPW security procedures.
  4. Visitors must remain accompanied by their designated host or an authorised security official while on the premises.
  5. In an emergency, visitors must follow the instructions of their host, security officials and emergency personnel.
  6. Private visits and unauthorised meetings are not permitted.
  7. Vehicles are parked at the owner’s risk, subject to applicable law.
  8. All visitors must comply with the rules governing prohibited and declarable items.

04

Declarable Items

🔫 Firearm
💻 Laptop
📷 Camera
📦 Private Property
💎 Valuables

Visitors must disclose applicable declarable items to security before entry. Security may inspect or record item details, including identifying information, in accordance with GPW procedures and applicable law. A firearm must be declared if present, but declaration does not override the prohibition on firearms or authorise entry with one. Security will determine the appropriate next steps under GPW policy.

05

POPIA Consent & Visitor Declaration

Access to GPW premises is subject to identity verification, security requirements and authorisation. GPW may refuse access in accordance with its policies and applicable law.

By voluntarily providing my SMS confirmation code to the security official, I confirm that I have had an opportunity to read and understand this notice, that I acknowledge the security procedures and agree to comply with them, and that I consent to the processing described above to the extent that consent is the applicable lawful basis. I understand that GPW may also process information where another lawful basis applies.

Confirm your declaration using the SMS code

A one-time code will be sent by SMS to the mobile number provided during registration. Only after reading this notice, and only if you agree, give that code to the security official. The official will enter it into the GPW visitor registration application to record your acknowledgement and, where applicable, consent. You do not need to enter the code on this website.
